ZERACK needs your microphone once

The part of ZERACK that listens runs out of sight, where Chrome cannot show its permission prompt, so it asks here, once. The permission belongs to the extension, so the ZERACK bubble can use it as soon as you allow it.

What you say is turned into text by Chrome's own speech recognition, the same service behind voice typing in Chrome. When Chrome cannot run it, a local Whisper model does the job on this computer instead, more slowly. The microphone is only open while you hold the bubble or the mic, press Alt+Z, or turn on Hands-free.
