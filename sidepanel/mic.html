ZERACK needs your microphone once

The part of ZERACK that listens runs out of sight, where Chrome cannot show its permission prompt, so it asks here, once. The permission belongs to the extension, so the ZERACK button can use it as soon as you allow it.

What you say is turned into text on this computer by a local Whisper model. The audio never leaves your machine.
