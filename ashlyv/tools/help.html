How the tools chain together to produce a faceless video end to end. They all use the free Groq or Gemini API key you set in Options. Voice runs on the browser synthesizer, no credits needed.

The recommended flow

1) NicheMaster: validate the niche (score, RPM, sub-niches, ideas). 2) RivalRadar: study the competition (searches, formats, gaps). 3) ScriptPilot: generate the script, scenes and title, description and tags. 4) VoxBatch: narrate the script with a browser voice, or export SSML. 5) MotionForge: image prompts per scene, plus thumbnails. 6) Command Center lands everything you save, scored and ranked.

ScriptPilot AI

Topic, niche, length and language become a continuous script, a scene breakdown and metadata ready to upload. Buttons hand the script to VoxBatch or MotionForge.

VoxBatch Pro

Paste the script, it splits into scenes and narrates them with the free browser voices. Pick voice, speed and pitch, and export the queue as SSML. The audio cannot be saved as a file from here: record it, or upload your own MP3 to Monetize Studio.

MotionForge Studio

A script becomes one image or video prompt per scene, written in English and ready for Flow, Veo, Midjourney or Nano Banana, with a consistent look, plus 3 high CTR thumbnail concepts.

RivalRadar Pro

A niche or keyword becomes searches that open YouTube in one click, the formats that work, the gaps nobody fills, monetization angles, RPM and saturation.

NicheMaster

A niche becomes an opportunity score from 0 to 100, RPM, demand, saturation, faceless fit, the richest sub-niches, video ideas that open in ScriptPilot, monetization routes and risks.

Nothing is being generated?

You need a free API key from Groq (console.groq.com) or Gemini (aistudio.google.com). Paste it in Options and come back.
