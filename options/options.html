ZERACK, Gemini API key
The AI coach runs on Google Gemini 1.5 Flash, free of charge: 15 requests a minute, 1500 a day, available in every region.
Get your API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Takes 30 seconds, no card needed.
Paid calls
Off by default. A scan can send up to twelve thumbnails to Gemini to judge whether a video is really faceless, and those calls count against your quota. Having a key stored is not permission to spend it.

Assistant model
Auto uses whichever provider you configured below and is answering. Pick a model to force it; if that provider is rate limited the request still falls through to the next one you configured.
Groq, the fast provider at 750 to 1000 tokens a second
Very fast cloud inference. 30 requests a minute, 14,400 a day, double what Gemini allows.
Create your key at console.groq.com/keys. Sign up with Google, no card needed.
Ollama, the local provider: no limits, works offline
AI models running on your machine. No limits, no internet needed. Slower than Groq, but nothing leaves the machine.
Download Ollama at ollama.com/download, then in a terminal run ollama pull llama3.2:3b
